# Summary mode

**Goal:** someone decides in 30 seconds whether this video is worth their time, and knows what it says if they never watch it.

Write this, in this order:

```markdown
# <Video title>: summary

> <One sentence: what this video gives the viewer.>

**Channel:** … · **Published:** … · **Length:** … · **Link:** …

## TL;DR
<Three bullet points, the three things worth remembering. Each with a timestamp.>

## Who should watch
<Who gets the most from it, and who can skip it.>

## Chapter by chapter
<For each section of the video (use its chapters if it has them): a heading with the timestamp, then 2–4 sentences on what is said or shown.>

## Best moments
<The 3 moments most worth watching directly, with timestamp links and why.>

## Verdict
<Watch / Skim / Skip, and one sentence why.>
```

Keep it under 600 words for a video under an hour, 900 for longer.
