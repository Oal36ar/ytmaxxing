# 🧠 Knowledge mode

**Goal:** a note an AI agent can search and trust months from now. Every useful idea in the video, structured and cited, nothing invented.

Write this:

```markdown
---
title: <video title>
channel: <channel>
url: <link>
published: <YYYY-MM-DD>
tags: [<5–10 lowercase topic tags>]
---

# <Video title>: knowledge

> <One sentence: the core idea of the video.>

## Key claims
| Claim | Type | Moment |
|---|---|---|
| <the claim in your words> | shown / measured / belief / story | [mm:ss](link) |

## Frameworks and mental models
<Each named or implied framework: what it is, how it works, when to use it, where it's explained.>

## Numbers worth keeping
<Every figure, price, metric or benchmark mentioned, with context and timestamp.>

## Tools, people and resources mentioned
<Name — what it is — why it came up — timestamp.>

## Where the speaker could be wrong
<Weak evidence, missing context, conflicts of interest, things that have likely changed since.>

## Open questions
<What the video raises but doesn't answer.>
```

Prefer more rows over longer rows. Skip a section only if the video truly has nothing for it.
